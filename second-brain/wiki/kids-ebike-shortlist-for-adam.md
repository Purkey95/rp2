# Kids' E-Bike Shortlist for Adam

**Question:** Which sub-$1,000 electric bike is right for Adam — 85 lb, about
4'5"–4'6", age 10–11 — for neighborhood and greenway riding around
Davidson/Huntersville, NC?

**Short answer:** None of the four verifiable candidates clears all of
[[kids-ebike-selection-criteria]]. The two machines that fit him cannot be
pedaled; the two that can be pedaled are 4+ inches too tall for him to touch
down on. The stated goal (pedaling on greenways) and the shortlist have been
pulling in opposite directions.

All specs verified against current listings on 2026-08-31.

## Fit analysis — the finding that decides it

A 53–54" child has a barefoot inseam of roughly 23–25". Seat height must be at
or below that for both feet flat at a stop.

- **Tuttio ARC-I — 23.4"** ✅ fits
- **Spacewalk M6 — 27.17–27.56"** ❌ ~3" too tall. The "23.23" seat-to-pedal"
  figure quoted earlier is a leg-extension measurement, not seat-to-floor.
- **Macfox M16 — 28.7"** ❌ ~4–5" too tall, despite Macfox's own size guide
  claiming 4'2"–5'0".
- **GOTRAX Fitz16** — seat height not published; must be measured in person.

## Candidates

### Tuttio ARC-I

47.4 lb, 23.4" seat, 750W, 36V 13Ah, 5 modes (7/10/13/17/22 mph), 150 lb max.

- **Throttle-only.** Child-sized twist throttle; no PAS, no gearing. Tuttio
  sells "ARC-I Foldable Pedals" as a separate accessory in its ARC Accessories
  collection, alongside a separate replacement throttle. **This corrects an
  earlier answer that said the ARC-I comes with pedals** — bolt-on pedals on a
  single-speed dirt-bike frame do not make it pedalable like a bicycle.
- **The limiter appears rider-unlockable.** Listings describe it as speed-
  limited to 15 mph "but can be unlocked as needed." The whole
  "keep him at 7–10 mph" plan depends on the limiter being *parent*-locked —
  confirm before buying.
- **Conflict:** Tuttio's own listings give ages 5–8, 5–13, and 8–13, and top
  speeds of 20, 22, and 28 mph. One Amazon listing is a 2-pack. No UL 2849
  claim found.
- Walmart's own listing title describes it for "Parking Lot Usage."

### GOTRAX Fitz16

44 lb, 350W, 36V 7.8Ah, 15.5 mph (2 modes: 10 / 15.5), 16" pneumatic tires,
dual suspension, rear disc brake, 220 lb capacity, **UL 2849**, ~$400–520.

Throttle-only, no pedals. But if the purchase is going to be a dirt bike
anyway, this beats the Tuttio on every axis that matters for a first machine:
lighter, certified, capped at 15.5 instead of 22 mph, and roughly $300 cheaper.
**Conflict:** listings give ages 8+ and 13+.

### Macfox M16

65.7 lb, 28.7" seat, 500W/750W peak, 20 mph, 48V 10.4Ah, 16×4 fat tires,
front+rear disc, 220 lb, **UL 2849, Class 2**, under $1,000.

The only candidate that is unambiguously a legal, certified e-bike with real
pedals and pedal assist. Fails the fit gate badly and is 77% of Adam's body
weight. Right category, wrong size.

### Spacewalk M6

(Eilleo SpaceWalk M6.) 750W, 48V 15Ah, 21.6 mph, 16×4 fat tires, four shock
absorbers, Shimano 6-speed with 152mm cranks, PAS.

Real pedals and real gearing — the most bicycle-like of the four.
**Conflict:** weight is listed as 49.6 lb on a current listing vs. the 74.96 lb
quoted earlier; unresolved. Marketed for "teens / riders under 5'2"", and its
27.17–27.56" seat height reflects that. Fails the fit gate.

## Legal context — NC greenways

North Carolina's three-class framework takes effect **December 1, 2026**:
Class 1 (pedal-assist, 20 mph), Class 2 (throttle, 20 mph), Class 3
(pedal-assist, 28 mph), each capped at 750W. Class-compliant e-bikes are
treated as bicycles on Charlotte-area greenways including Little Sugar Creek,
the Cross Charlotte Trail, and Carolina Thread Trail segments.

A throttle-only machine with no functional pedals and a 22 mph top speed fits
**no class** — it is a motor vehicle, so it belongs on private property, not on
greenways or public roads. That rules the Tuttio and the Fitz16 out of the
originally stated use case. NC also requires helmets for riders under 16.

## Recommendation

**Decide the category first; the answer follows from it.**

1. **If the goal is greenways and pedaling** (what was originally asked for):
   don't buy from this shortlist. Adam is exactly the right size for a quality
   **24"-wheel non-electric mountain/hybrid bike** now. If it must be electric,
   look at 24"-wheel youth e-bikes with adjustable seatposts rather than
   16"-wheel moto-style frames — the 16" frames put the seat high and fixed.
2. **If the goal is an off-road machine for a dirt lot or private property:**
   the **GOTRAX Fitz16 at ~$450–520** is the better buy over the Tuttio ARC-I
   at $780–860. 350W is ample for an 85 lb rider.

**Next action:** measure Adam's barefoot inseam against a wall. That single
number resolves the fit gate for every bike on the list, and it's the number to
carry into a shop.

### The sizing catch

REI is the only retailer here that publishes an **inseam** range rather than a
height range: the REV 24 is specified for a **24–28" inseam**. Adam's estimated
23–25" puts him at the very bottom of the 24"-wheel window — so a 20"-wheel
bike may still be correct. This is the conversation to have in a shop, not
online, and it reinforces that the inseam measurement comes first.
